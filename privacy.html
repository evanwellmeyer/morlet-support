Privacy Policy

Morlet: Sound Spectrum & Wavelets

Effective October 13, 2026

Morlet does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet.

Microphone

Morlet asks for microphone access so it can analyze and display sound in real time. Audio is processed on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. When you are not recording, audio is analyzed and immediately discarded.

Recordings

When you choose to record, Morlet saves the audio and its analysis data in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Recordings leave your device only if you share them yourself, using the share button. You can delete recordings in the app at any time, and all of them are removed if you delete the app.

Settings

Your analysis and display preferences are saved on your iPhone so they are remembered between launches. They are never transmitted.

Children

Morlet collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
